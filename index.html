Legal & Privacy

LIA is an AI voice & chat assistant for Android, made by codeninjavik. This page indexes every policy document for the app. It applies to both editions of LIA - the full edition distributed from codeninjavik.in, and the Google Play edition, which has a smaller feature set (see the Privacy Policy for the exact differences).

Quick answers

Does LIA sell your data?No. Never.
Does LIA listen in the background?No, except when you turn on "Auto Listening" yourself in Settings - see the Privacy Policy.
Who processes your voice/chat?Voice goes to Google's Gemini Live API; typed chat goes to Groq (or Gemini as a fallback), to generate LIA's replies. See §3 of the Privacy Policy.
Does the Play edition use Accessibility?No - that edition has no screen-automation or social-posting features at all.
How do I delete my data?Delete conversations from History or Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device. See §7 of the Privacy Policy.
